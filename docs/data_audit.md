## 8. What this changes for the model

- **Speed is m/s, whatever the metadata says.** The metadata XML labels the speed channel `kph`; DATA.md says m/s. Reported speed over displacement speed between consecutive frames has median 0.989, so the values are m/s and the label is wrong. Use the `s` channel as m/s, without conversion (`docs/decisions.md` D-008).
- **The ball's speed channel is not usable as-is.** It is present on only 8.7% of ball-seen frames and its ratio to displacement speed is 0.308 -- undocumented semantics. kloppy drops it entirely; `src.data_loader` keeps it, but no feature should rely on it without a separate investigation.
- **The official baseline's two parsing assumptions hold** (slot order, home team), and `src.data_loader` matches the official parser on every frame and kloppy on every sampled value (section 4). The four committed baselines are an exact regression target for the loader.
- **The ball is missing on ~41% of graded frames** (published figures reproduced exactly). A missing ball is normal input, never on its own evidence of a dead ball.
- **Tracked objects do not vanish outside play.** Mean tracked objects: 12.79 before kickoff, 12.51 at halftime, 14.53 after the final whistle, against 20.5 during graded play. A head count cannot find halftime; the published half blocks must (`games.py`).
- **The ball leaves the pitch rectangle.** 95.8% of graded ball positions lie inside [0,1]^2, so roughly 4.2% are outside the touchlines or goal lines: a candidate dead-ball signal for Phase 2, to be validated without the public GT.
- **Homography figures differ from DATA.md's descriptive numbers** (median reprojection error 0.23 on graded frames vs 0.24 published for "during play"; 0.39 over the halftime block vs "about 1.5" -- the halftime *mean* is 1.5, so the published figure is most likely a mean). DATA.md does not define either frame range. The homography is not used by the Phase-1 pipeline, so this is recorded, not acted on.

## 9. Risks and limitations

- Published figures are the yardstick. A PASS means the bundle matches what the organisers describe, not that the organisers' description is complete.
- `games.py` kickoff anchors are playback positions (`anchor_video_frame` gives 11186 and 113741), not contract frames. The contract frames are 11185 (first graded frame) and 113742 (2nd-half kickoff, FAQ.md); frames 110808..113741 are graded and all `D` (FAQ.md).
- Smart Tagging intervals are windows around events (kickoff tags open ~3.5 s early, starter notebook 01); only containment was checked, never exact boundaries.
- Videos were not decoded or hashed (pass `--hash-videos` to hash them); nothing in Phase 1 reads video.
- The broadcast recording is on its own timeline. It was audited for internal consistency only and is never compared frame-for-frame with tactical.
- The local bundle sits in capitalised `Tactical/` and `Broadcast/` folders; `games.py` asks for lowercase. That resolves on Windows (case-insensitive) but not on Linux (`docs/decisions.md` D-004).
